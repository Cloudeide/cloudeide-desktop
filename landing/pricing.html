Questions and answers

What is a credit?

One credit is one US dollar of usage. Each model uses credits at its own rate, per million tokens, shown above. Expensive models use your credits faster; cheap ones make them last.

What happens when my plan's credits run out?

The agent carries on with credits you have bought. When both are used up it stops until your plan renews or you buy more. It never charges you more than you have.

Do credits I buy expire?

No. Credits you buy stay in your account until you use them. Your plan's credits renew every month.

Is hosting included?

Yes. You can deploy from the editor on every plan, with a preview link first, production when you allow it, your own domain and roll back. Each deploy uses 0.05 credits, and storage is included up to your plan's limit. On Free, hosting is a 14-day trial.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. Upgrade or change plans from Plan and billing in your account.

How does CloudeIDE use my code?

Your files stay on your computer. What the agent reads to do a job is sent to the model that answers it. The Data Use Policy has the details.

Where can I ask more questions?

Write to support@cloudeide.com.
